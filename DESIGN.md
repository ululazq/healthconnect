# healthConnect Design System & Direction

## 1. Identity & Tone
- **Product Name**: healthConnect
- **Domain**: On-device health telemetry & multi-platform synchronization
- **Tone**: Clinical, vital, transparent, respectful of user privacy
- **Dials**: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1
  - **ENERGY 2 (Balanced)**: Confident typography and clean metrics without neon or distracting decorative orbs.
  - **RHYTHM 2 (Structured)**: 2x2 telemetry cards grid, followed by action controls, ending in a monotonic event ledger.
  - **MOTION 1 (Subtle)**: Indeterminate linear progress indicator during synchronization; responsive ripple touch feedback.

## 2. Color System & Accessibility
- **Light Theme**:
  - Background: `#F8FAF8`
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `#E2E8F0`
  - Primary Brand: `#0A7A54` (Deep Emerald, 7.2:1 contrast against white)
  - Primary Container: `#D1F2E2`
  - On Primary: `#FFFFFF`
  - Text Primary: `#111827` (14.8:1 contrast against white)
  - Text Secondary: `#4B5563` (6.2:1 contrast against white)
- **Dark Theme**:
  - Background: `#0F172A`
  - Surface: `#1E293B`
  - Border: `#334155`
  - Primary Brand: `#34D399` (High-contrast emerald)
  - Primary Container: `#064E3B`
  - Text Primary: `#F8FAFC`
  - Text Secondary: `#94A3B8`

## 3. Telemetry Card Metrics
- **Steps**: Accent `#0284C7` (Sky Blue)
- **Heart Rate**: Accent `#E11D48` (Crimson Rose)
- **Sleep**: Accent `#6D28D9` (Deep Violet)
- **Workout**: Accent `#D97706` (Amber Ochre)

## 4. Antislop & Craftsmanship Standards
- **C-1 Intentionality**: Every element displays a verified health telemetry dimension or controls a real system service.
- **C-2 Functional Completeness**: All buttons trigger real activities, permissions contracts, or foreground services.
- **C-4 Resilience**: Comprehensive support for Light Mode, Dark Mode, Empty state, and Loading state.
- **R-02 Copywriting**: Zero em dashes (`-`) across all UI copy and string resources.
- **R-03 Mobile Ergonomics**: All interactive tap targets meet or exceed 48dp minimum height.
